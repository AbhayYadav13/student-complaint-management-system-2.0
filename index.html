Institutional Grievance Policy & Redressal Flow

Our multi-tiered grievance redressal mechanism ensures that every concern submitted by a student receives prompt administrative attention. Complaints are acknowledged immediately with a unique Complaint ID, evaluated by departmental grievance coordinators, and resolved in accordance with institutional charters.
